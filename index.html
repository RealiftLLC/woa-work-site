05 — How the shoe works

Comfort alone does not fix it. Comfort plus support does.

A soft cushioned shoe feels good in the store and lets the foot collapse by month two. A stiff shoe stabilizes the foot but punishes it on hard, man-made floors. Foot fatigue goes down when one shoe does both: cushioning and support.

#1

Comfort.

A removable cork-and-foam insole (comfort & support) under a full-grain leather upper shapes to the wearer’s feet.

#2

Support.

A reinforced heel counter and nylon shank keep the heel upright and the arch from rolling in as the hours add up.

#3

Work-ready.

Slip-resistant outsole. Professional styles that look as good as they feel.

#4

Fit.

Every pair is made to order from the employee's own scan: eight measurements per foot, taken on their phone in 30 seconds. Left and right can be different sizes and widths, because most feet are. If it doesn’t fit right, we remake them for free.

#5

Style.

This isn’t a boring work shoe. Our sophisticated styles will appeal to your employees and provide higher satisfaction.

09 — FAQs

Questions leaders ask.

What evidence is there that shoes affect turnover?
The research links standing work to foot pain, and foot pain to lost hours and job changes. No study we know of isolates a shoe's effect on retention.
What does it cost?
Under the Discount Partnership, about $46 per participating employee on a $185 pair. You set a cap on participation.
Which budget does this come from?
Customers typically look at uniform, retention, wellness or safety budgets.
How do you fit hundreds of people without a fitting day?
Each employee does a 30-second scan in their phone's browser. We build their pair from eight measurements per foot.
What if a pair does not fit?
Every pair comes with a 30-day wear period, free returns and a free remake. Our return rate is 3.85%, against an industry average of 18.5%.
Are they slip resistant?
Yes. The outsole has been independently tested to ASTM F2913.
How much work is this for our team?
For the Discount Partnership, sharing a link and approving an invoice. We handle scanning, fulfillment, returns, and support. You handle marketing (emails) to your employees.
